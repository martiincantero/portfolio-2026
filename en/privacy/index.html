MC / Privacy

Privacy, in plain language.

This portfolio has no analytics, advertising pixels or forms that submit data to a proprietary backend.

Language preference

When you choose English or Spanish, your preference is saved in your browser’s local storage (mc-language). It is only used to remember your language on your next visit. You can remove it in your browser settings.

Motion preference

You can reduce motion from the footer. This choice is stored locally (mc-motion). Your system accessibility preference is always respected. Nothing is sent to a server.

Email and external links

The contact link opens your email application. Copy email only accesses the clipboard when you press the button. GitHub and LinkedIn are external services with their own privacy policies.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. The initial configuration is prepared for GitHub Pages. This description should be reviewed if services are added or hosting is changed.

Interactive examples

The availability simulation runs in your browser. It does not save time slots, transmit data or create bookings.

Contact

For any questions about this website, email martincantero.it@hotmail.com.
